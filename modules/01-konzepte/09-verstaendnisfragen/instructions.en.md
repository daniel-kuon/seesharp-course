# Comprehension questions

This level contains hardly any code, so this quiz mostly asks about the concepts: class and object, encapsulation, namespace and assembly, IL, JIT, CLR and BCL. If needed, re-read in the book **1.3 Object-oriented programming** (page 9), **1.5 Start class and Main()** (page 12), **2.2 .NET implementations** (page 21), **2.3 Compiler and IL** (page 25), **2.4 Assemblies** (page 30), **2.5 CLR** (page 42) and **2.6 BCL** (page 47).

The few code snippets are small; describe what they do and why. The multiple-choice questions have exactly one correct answer. The AI tutor judges the open answers (it gives you one round of corrections), after which your supervisor reviews everything. Two to five sentences in your own words are enough.
