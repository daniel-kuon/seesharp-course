# Operator Quiz

Read Section **4.5**, "**Operators and Expressions"** (page 215), in the book: 4.5.1 Arithmetic Operators (page 216), 4.5.5 Logical Operators (page 225), 4.5.8 Assignment Operators (page 232), 4.5.9 Conditional Operator (page 235), and 4.5.10 Order of Evaluation with the Operator Table (page 236).

First, calculate the expressions in your head, then verify them in a small console project. Your answers should be based on your understanding of the order of operations, not on the output.
