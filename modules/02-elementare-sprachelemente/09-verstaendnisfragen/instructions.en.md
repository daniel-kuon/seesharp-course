# Comprehension questions

This quiz checks whether you can not only apply the basic language elements but also explain them. If needed, re-read in the book **4.2.3 Formatted output** (page 176), **4.3.4 Primitive data types** (page 186), **4.3.5 Floating-point numbers in memory** (page 189), **4.5.1 Arithmetic operators** (page 216), **4.5.7 Type conversion** (page 229) and **4.5.10 Order of evaluation** (page 236).

For the questions with code, work out the answer in your head first and check it afterwards in a small console project. The multiple-choice questions have exactly one correct answer. The AI tutor judges the open answers (it gives you one round of corrections), after which your supervisor reviews everything.
