# Comprehension questions: LINQ

This quiz checks whether you can explain the ideas behind LINQ and read unfamiliar queries. If needed, re-read **18.2.4 Deferred or Immediate Execution** (page 1215), **18.2.8.2 SelectMany** (page 1220), **18.2.9 GroupBy** (page 1222), **18.2.10 Linking Two Sequences** (page 1224), and **18.3 Query Syntax** (page 1235).

For the "What does this code do?" questions, say what is printed before you run anything, and give your reasons; feel free to try it out afterwards. Open answers are judged first by the AI tutor (one correction round); the supervisor then reviews them with you.
