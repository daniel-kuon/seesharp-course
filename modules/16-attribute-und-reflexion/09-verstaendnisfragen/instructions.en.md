# Comprehension questions: Attributes and Reflection

This quiz checks whether you can explain attributes and reflection and read unfamiliar code. If needed, re-read **14.1 Applying Attributes** (page 922), **14.2 Evaluating Attributes via Reflection** (page 924), **14.5 Further BCL Attributes** (pages 934 to 940), and **14.6 Defining Attributes** (page 940).

For the "What does this code do?" questions, say what is printed before you run anything, and give your reasons; feel free to try it out afterwards. Open answers are judged first by the AI tutor (one correction round); the supervisor then reviews them with you.
