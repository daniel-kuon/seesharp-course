# Comprehension questions: Multithreading

This quiz checks whether you can explain threads, synchronization, tasks, and `async`/`await` and read unfamiliar code. If needed, re-read **17.2.4 Coordinating Access** (page 1072, especially the `lock` statement in 17.2.4.1.1 on page 1073), **17.5 Tasks** (page 1118, especially 17.5.6 on page 1129 and 17.5.10 on page 1145), and **17.6 async and await** (page 1161, especially 17.6.12).

For the "What does this code do?" questions, say what is printed before you run anything, and give your reasons; feel free to try it out afterwards. Open answers are judged first by the AI tutor (one correction round); the supervisor then reviews them with you.
