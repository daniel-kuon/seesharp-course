# Comprehension questions: Entity Framework Core

This quiz checks whether you understand what EF Core does behind the scenes, not just which methods exist. If needed, re-read
**20.4 The DbContext** (page 1414), **20.9 Queries with LINQ** including 20.9.7 on `Include` (pages 1451 to 1464),
**20.10 and 20.11 Changing and Deleting** (pages 1466 to 1478) and **20.13 and 20.14 Conflicts and Transactions** (pages 1482 to 1490).

For the code questions you say what the program prints and give your reasons. The AI tutor judges open answers
when you submit them (with one round of correction); afterwards your supervisor reviews your answers.
