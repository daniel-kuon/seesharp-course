# Comprehension questions: WPF and MVVM

This quiz checks whether you understood the ideas behind XAML, data binding and MVVM. If needed, re-read
**12.4 to 12.6 XAML and code-behind** (page 746), **12.8 and 12.9 routed events and dependency properties** (pages 766 to 786),
**12.10 data binding** (pages 787 to 803) and **12.13 MVVM** including 12.13.3 command binding (pages 856 to 883).

The code questions are plain C# without a user interface: say what the program prints and give your reasons. The AI tutor judges open answers
when you submit them (with one round of correction); afterwards your supervisor reviews your answers.
