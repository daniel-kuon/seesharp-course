# Networking Terms: Quiz

Read **section 21.1 “Network Technology Terms”** (page 1497) in the book, including **21.1.1 “OSI Model”** (page 1498) and **21.1.2 “How Protocol Stacks Work”** (page 1503), as well as the beginning of **21.3 Using Internet Resources via HttpClient** (page 1506) along with **21.3.1 Request Methods** (page 1507).

The questions do not require any programming. In the book's sample folder, `Netzwerk/HTTPS/HttpMethod.Head` shows what a `HEAD` request returns.
