# Comprehension questions: Publishing

This quiz checks whether you understood the decisions involved in publishing, not just the commands. If needed, re-read
**23.1 Publishing Options** (page 1637) including 23.1.4 Target Runtime (page 1643), 23.1.5 Deployment Mode (page 1644),
23.1.6 and 23.1.7 on single file and ReadyToRun (pages 1646 to 1648) and **23.2.3 Web Application** (pages 1658 to 1662).

For the command questions you say what is produced or what happens and give your reasons. The AI tutor judges open answers
when you submit them (with one round of correction); afterwards your supervisor reviews your answers.
